How to play Underscoredle

  1. Count the underscores in the string. Each round has between 1 and 30.
  2. Use the single sample underscore as a size reference.
  3. Enter your count and select “Check guess” or press Enter.
  4. Use the higher or lower hints and keep guessing until you get it right.

Select “New round” whenever you want a fresh string.
